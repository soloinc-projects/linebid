Getting started

How do I set up my first estimate?

1. Edit your rate catalog: Open LineBid and tap the "Rate Catalog" tab. You'll see electrical items with pre-filled labor and material costs. Update these to match your local market rates.

2. Start a new estimate: Tap "New Estimate" and name the job (e.g., "Smith residence, kitchen renovation").

3. Add quantities: Tap the "+" next to each item (outlets, switches, breakers, etc.) to add how many the job needs. The total updates in real time.

4. Review and export: Tap "Review" to see the line-by-line breakdown. Tap "Export PDF" to download a customer-ready estimate.

Can I change the rates in my catalog?

Yes. Go to the "Rate Catalog" tab and tap any item to edit its labor cost, material cost, or description. Your changes apply to all future estimates.

To apply an updated rate to a past estimate you've already saved, you'll need to edit that estimate's quantities to refresh its calculations.

Where are my estimates saved?

All estimates are saved locally on your device. They don't go anywhere else — no cloud, no server, no account. If you enable iCloud backup on your device (Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > iCloud Backup), iOS can optionally back up LineBid's data, but LineBid itself never controls or uploads your data.

How do I export an estimate as a PDF?

Open or create an estimate, tap "Review," and then tap "Export PDF." Your device's share sheet opens. You can save the PDF to Files, email it, print it, or share it however you like.

Common questions

Does LineBid use AI to calculate prices?

No. LineBid has no artificial intelligence. Every number in your estimate comes from the labor and material rates in your catalog, which you control. If the rate is $50/hour, that's what shows up in the total.

Is there a monthly subscription?

No. LineBid is a one-time $9.99 purchase. Once you buy the Pro Unlock, it's yours forever. No recurring charges, no data paywalls, no surprises.

Do I need an account to use LineBid?

No account required. Open the app, edit your rates, and create an estimate. Everything you do stays on your device.

What if I want to add a trade besides electrical?

LineBid v1 covers electrical work only. If you'd like to estimate plumbing, HVAC, or other trades, reach out to support@8086.llc with your request — future versions may expand the catalog based on user feedback.

Can I add custom items to my rate catalog?

The v1 catalog is pre-filled with common electrical items, and you can edit the rates for those items. Adding entirely new items to the catalog is not supported in v1 but may be added later.

What happens if I delete LineBid?

Deleting the app deletes all your estimates and rate catalog since they are stored only on your device. If you've exported estimates as PDFs, those files remain on your device or where you saved them (email, cloud storage, etc.).

Which iOS version does LineBid need?

LineBid requires iOS 26.0 or later. It will not run on older versions of iOS.

Does LineBid work offline?

Yes, completely. LineBid never needs an internet connection. All calculations and data stay on your device.

Privacy and data

Where can I find the privacy policy?

Visit the Privacy page for the full details on how LineBid handles your data.

How do I delete all my data?

On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings > Apps > LineBid > Remove App > Delete App. All your estimates and rate catalog are deleted immediately since they're stored only on your device.

If you've saved PDFs to other apps or email, you'll need to delete those separately in those apps.
