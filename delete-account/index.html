Account & Data Deletion

No account required

LineBid doesn't use accounts, usernames, passwords, or sign-in of any kind. There is nothing for us to delete on a server because LineBid has no server and stores nothing about you anywhere except on your device.

Deleting your data

Since all your data is stored only on your device, deleting your data is simple:

  1. Open Settings on your iPhone or iPad
  2. Tap Apps (or Application Manager on some devices)
  3. Find LineBid in the list
  4. Tap "Remove App" then "Delete App"
  5. Confirm by tapping "Delete"

That's it. All your estimates, rate catalog, and any other data LineBid stored are deleted immediately from your device. They are never backed up elsewhere, so deletion is permanent.

What gets deleted

  • Your rate catalog (labor and material costs)
  • All saved estimates and quantities
  • Any locally stored configuration

What doesn't get deleted

  • PDFs you've exported: If you've exported estimates as PDFs and saved them to your Files app, email, or another service, those remain. You can delete them manually from those apps.
  • iCloud backup: If you have iCloud backup enabled on your device and LineBid data was included in a backup, that backup remains on iCloud. To remove it, disable iCloud Backup for LineBid (Settings > [Your Name] > iCloud > Manage Storage > LineBid).

Questions about your data?

If you have concerns about your data or how LineBid handles it, see the Privacy Policy or email support@8086.llc.

LineBid keeps your data on your device. Deletion is your choice, under your control.
