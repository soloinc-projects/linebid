Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 17, 2026
Last updated: October 17, 2026
Language: English (US)

What LineBid collects

Nothing.

LineBid has no account, no sign-in, and makes no network calls of its own. All data you create — the labor and material rates you edit, and the quantities and totals you calculate for each estimate — is stored only in a local file on your device and never transmitted anywhere by the app.

Where your data lives

Your rate catalog and saved estimates are stored in the app's own local storage on your device (CatalogStore, a JSON file in iOS's app-sandboxed container). This file is encrypted by iOS at the device level when you enable device lock.

If you have iCloud backup enabled on your device, iOS can back up this app's data to your iCloud account at your option — but that backup is your own account, not something LineBid can access or see. LineBid itself never touches your iCloud account.

What third parties might see

  • Apple: Handles the purchase of the Pro Unlock feature through App Store In-App Purchase. Apple collects the minimum information needed for that transaction (your purchase record) as governed by Apple's own privacy policy. LineBid does not add any data collection beyond what that purchase requires.
  • No analytics, no ads, no third-party SDKs: LineBid contains no analytics library, no advertising network, no crash-reporting SDK, and no other third-party code. The entire app is built on Swift's standard library, Foundation, and Apple's system frameworks only.

Data deletion

Deleting LineBid from your device deletes all of its local data instantly, since none of it exists anywhere else. There is no server, no account, and no database for LineBid to retain it.

How to delete all your data: On your iPhone, go to Settings > Apps > LineBid > Remove App > Delete App. Your data is gone immediately.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of LineBid adds a network feature (such as cloud backup of your rate catalog or sharing estimates with a team), this privacy policy will be updated to describe that feature honestly before it ships.

Questions or concerns

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how your data is handled in LineBid, visit the Support page for contact information.

LineBid is built for transparency and control. Your data, your estimates, always.
